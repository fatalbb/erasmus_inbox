Q: When will I receive my Erasmus grant?
A: 70% is paid within 30 days after we have your signed Learning Agreement and your Certificate of Arrival. The remaining 30% is paid after we receive your Transcript of Records.

Q: When must I send my Certificate of Arrival?
A: Within 7 days of arriving, signed and stamped by your host university.

Q: What insurance do I need?
A: Health insurance valid in your host country (the European Health Insurance Card is enough inside the EU), plus accident and liability insurance for your whole stay.

Q: How do I change my Learning Agreement?
A: Fill in the "Changes during the mobility" section, get it signed by you and both coordinators, and send it to us within 5 weeks of the start of your semester.

Q: Can I extend my stay?
A: Ask your host and home coordinators, then send us the signed extension request at least one month before your current end date.

Q: When is the Erasmus office open?
A: Monday to Thursday, 10:00 to 12:00, room B-104, or email erasmus@example.edu.
